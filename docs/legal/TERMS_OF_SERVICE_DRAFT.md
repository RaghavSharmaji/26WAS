# WareFlow Terms of Service — Draft for Review

> **Not approved for commercial use.** This draft is a product placeholder, not legal advice, and must be reviewed and approved by the service operator and qualified counsel before acceptance can be treated as a binding commercial contract.

## Workspace and accounts

WareFlow provides company workspaces for warehouse-management activities. Users must submit accurate information, protect account credentials, and access only workspaces and records authorized for their assigned role. A company application is pending until reviewed and activated by a platform administrator.

## Customer records

The customer is responsible for the accuracy and lawful use of company, supplier, product, inventory, order, employee, and other records entered into its workspace. Registration estimates do not create warehouses or operational records.

## Availability and support

This draft does not define uptime, support commitments, service credits, pricing, data retention, suspension notice, or termination. Those terms must be agreed and documented before commercial operation.

Application acceptance currently records version `wareflow-terms-draft-1`. This version label does not imply legal review or approval.
